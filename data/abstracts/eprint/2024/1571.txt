In this document we discuss the soundness of the Basefold multilinear polynomial commitment scheme [Zeilberger, Chen, Fisch 23] applied to Reed-Solomon codes, and run with proximity parameters up to the Johnson list decoding bound. Our security analysis relies on a generalization of the celebrated correlated agreement theorem from [Ben-Sasson, et al., 20] to linear subcodes of Reed-Solomon codes, which turns out a by-product of the Guruswami-Sudan list decoder analysis. We further highlight a non-linear variant of the subcode correlated agreement theorem, which is flexible enough to apply to Basefold-like protocols such as a row-batched version of FRI-Binius [Diamond, Posen 24] and WHIR [Arnon, Chiesa, Fenzi, Yogev 24] in the list-decoding regime.
