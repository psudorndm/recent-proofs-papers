We present a 4-round statistical non-malleable zero-knowledge (statistical NMZK) argument for NP in the plain model under standard hardness assumptions. Our construction can be based on any collision-resistant hash function and injective one-way function, and it guarantees simulation extractability in the delayed-input, one-many setting. Before this work, 4-round constructions were known for computational NMZK but not for statistical NMZK.
