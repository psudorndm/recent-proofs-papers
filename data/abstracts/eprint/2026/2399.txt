Hair and Sahai (arXiv:2609.18275v1) have recently proposed a construction of witness encryption for NP satisfying semantic security. In this note we observe that this recent witness-encryption construction is unconditionally extractable in the generic group model. This yields a construction of extractable witness encryption without assuming subexponential soundness of SNARGs, in contrast to the recent work of Jin (ePrint 2026/2063), which requires this assumption. The result in this note, moreover, complements the concurrent work of Chen and Vaikuntanathan (ePrint 2026/2346), who show the same construction to be public-coin extractable under a knowledge-style assumption; our argument is unconditional in the GGM.
