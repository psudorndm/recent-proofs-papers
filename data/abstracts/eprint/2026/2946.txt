Blind SNARKs combine fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) with cryptographic proofs to enable verifiable computation over encrypted data, and can substantially outperform SNARK-over-FHE approaches in efficiency. However, ciphertext openings in existing constructions can inflate proofs to tens or hundreds of megabytes. We present BOCCHI, a compact and efficient blind SNARK that addresses this bottleneck by committing to ciphertexts natively and opening them through a few aggregates. To establish soundness even for malformed ciphertexts, we introduce canonical binding and a lightweight noise filter that achieves it with optimal error, tools that may be of independent interest. We further formulate this transformation as a Commit–Aggregate–Check (CAC) compiler and show its effectiveness on two systems with different PIOPs (GKR and Fractal). At $2^{22}$ constraints, BOCCHI produces a 6.57 MB proof in 115.3 seconds, 9.3× smaller and 2.54× faster than Phalanx. Its proof is the smallest among five baselines. Our implementation is available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/BOCCHI-Artifacts.
